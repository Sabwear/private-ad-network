# Pilot and Operations Plan

## Shape

- 5-10 complementary businesses
- 5-15 screens in one compact service area
- Four operational weeks after technical stabilization
- One standardized Android TV box model plus spare units
- At least two approved ads per advertiser and network fallback media
- Starter credits with no screen/time multipliers

## Partner selection

Prioritize visible screens and meaningful dwell time: cafes, salons, gyms, waiting rooms, service centers, coworking, hotels, and retail counters. Avoid a network dominated by direct competitors.

Record category, operating hours, placement, screen size, traffic band, audio policy, category exclusions, and support contact for every location.

## Installation checklist

- Confirm screen is visible from the intended customer area
- Document placement without capturing audience identity
- Verify HDMI input, power recovery, Wi-Fi stability, and ventilation
- Pair device to the correct location
- Verify app autostart/lock-task behavior
- Download test manifest and checksum-valid media
- Run an accepted 30-second test settlement
- Test router disconnect and event synchronization
- Confirm owner support route and admin exit method

## Weekly review metrics

- Active-screen rate
- Verified minutes per screen/day
- Campaign fill and under-delivery
- Credit earn/spend ratio by member
- Crash-free playback and offline recovery
- Held/rejected/disputed play rate
- Support incidents and time-to-resolution
- Partner retention, referrals, and paid-package intent

## Runbooks

### Screen offline

Stop earning immediately, apply grace/alert policy, contact venue, use remote diagnostics, walk through power/input/network checks, and replace hardware if unresolved.

### Prohibited ad

Pause campaign, invalidate future manifests, preserve evidence, document reason, notify affected partners, and complete legal/compliance escalation.

### Ledger dispute

Inspect manifest/events/signals/policy, record decision authority and reason, issue a linked reversal when warranted, and respond within the published deadline.

### Platform outage

Publish status, allow valid cached playback under policy, hold uncertain settlement, restore services, reconcile evidence/idempotency, and communicate resolution.

### Compromised device/account

Revoke credentials, suspend scoped earning/campaigns, preserve logs, rotate secrets, investigate related sessions, and require controlled reactivation.

## Exit criteria for paid launch

- Reliability targets met for four consecutive weeks
- No unresolved unexplained wallet imbalance or duplicate settlement
- Contracts, content terms, privacy notice, refunds, tax treatment, and moderation rules approved
- Backups and restore test documented
- Installation, replacement, suspension, and support processes proven
- At least one real paid package tested
- Support can explain every charged play through evidence and policy version
